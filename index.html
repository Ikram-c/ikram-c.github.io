Ikram Choudhury

I am a Data Scientist and AI/ML Solutions Specialist with a deep focus on computer vision applications. Currently operating as a free agent, I can’t help but continue to collaborate, learn, and face both the human challenges computer vision aims to assist with, and the herculean technical hurdles of bringing it to life.

My most recent formal work was an incredible two-and-a-half years with marine scientists at Cefas. There, I programmed end-to-end MLOps pipelines processing terabyte and petabyte-scale datasets for national and international projects. Beyond overhauling the UK's Benthic AI litter project and presenting at the ICES conference, I expanded into RAG systems, large-scale NLP, and worked alongside world-class statisticians to implement advanced methods like Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) models for causal analysis.

Before Cefas, I worked as a Data Engineer Consultant, writing production flight analysis code for British Airways. My technical foundation comes from an MSc in Space Engineering and a BEng in Aerospace Engineering. Working with satellite radar data and UAV aerodynamics seamlessly transitioned into my current focus on Earth observation and scalable geospatial models.

When I'm not optimizing AI models or building cloud-based video analysis architectures like my Cloud-DashCam-POC, I enjoy exploring UI design—a skill I inadvertently picked up while creating comics with 3D animation and CAD tools during my adolescence.

In my free time, I write informal blogs on AI, document my research methods, and solo travel across the UK and abroad.

Exploring the Site

Beyond my formal roles, I use this site to host some of my ongoing side projects and raw ideas. You'll find two main sections linked in the navigation:

  • AberTech POCs: This started as a personal sandbox for testing out various proofs of concept. Over time, it evolved into a foundational draft for an AI company designed to assist with search and rescue missions in mountainous terrain.
  • The Cutting Room Floor: Not every experiment makes it to production. This space is a scrapbook for draft notes and code snippets from ML experiments I've toyed with in my spare time—like attempting natural language processing by analyzing the whitespaces rather than the words themselves. It’s an unfiltered mix ranging from pages of raw research notes to partially working code and algebraic breakdowns that I plan to revisit.
