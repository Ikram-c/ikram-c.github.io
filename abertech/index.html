Welcome

AI for assisting Search and Rescue.

In mountainous and difficult-to-navigate terrain, rescue teams are tracking a needle in a haystack where every second counts. AberTech leverages a three-pillared approach—UAV imaging data, ESA satellite data, and automotive dashcam tracking—to improve response times without drastically adding to operational costs.

Inspired by 18,000 miles of field observation through Wales and Scotland, these tools are built to be simple enough for volunteer teams who can't dedicate significant hours to learning programming skills. It is a retrofitted solution aimed at improving teamwork and human aid.

About AberTech

AI is no job for a single person.

Following extensive travels through regions like Snowdonia and the Scottish Highlands, and leveraging a background with Cefas and Defra, it became clear that search and rescue teams face monumental challenges. Funding is a continuous bottleneck, and the terrain is too vast for standard responses.

AberTech is dedicated to unifying UAV, satellite, and dashcam data to assist these volunteers. As a solo venture transitioning into a collaborative effort, I am actively seeking feedback and partnerships. We need tools that are cost-effective, simple to train on, and built strictly to improve teamwork and human aid.

The Implementation Strategy
  1. Integrate ESA satellite data to extract terrain, vegetation, and agriculture bands.
  2. Adapt Uber's H3 hexagonal models to land topography to map efficient search pathways.
  3. Chunk and stream automotive dashcam footage to identify missing person "near-misses".
  4. Deploy UAV anomaly detection (e.g., Parrot systems) for high-resolution spotting in restrictive terrain.
  5. Ensure all tools require minimal programming skills for volunteer operators.
  6. Focus strictly on retrofitted, low-cost solutions to bypass persistent funding bottlenecks.

Cloud-Optimised CCTV
Supporting infrastructure. Search a week of footage, download only the minutes you need. Movement-aware OME-Zarr archival with chunk-level cloud querying.
Dashcam Tracking (Pillar 1)
Chunking and streaming vehicle video data into OME-Zarr formats based on network coverage, designed to check dashcam footage for any "near misses" a search and rescue team may have with a target.
HexCover Mapping (Pillar 2)
Adapting Uber's H3 hexagonal models and ESA satellite data to complex land topography. The goal is to isolate pathways from a last known sighting and direct SAR services efficiently.
UAV Anomaly Detection (Pillar 3)
Transitioning unsupervised visual counting into human anomaly detection. Identifying targets that appear as little more than a few pixels on high-resolution UAV imagery to aid volunteer rescue teams.
Vision Toolkit
The underlying image QC libraries being developed in PlumImageTools and consolidated into one central monorepo to support the UAV and Dashcam pillars.
