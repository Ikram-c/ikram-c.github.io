The Initiative

Rescue, no matter what is in your way.

In time-critical alpine emergencies, the operational window for live recovery deteriorates rapidly within minutes[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span)[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span). AberTech is an orchestration layer built to resolve this by decoupling the operational objectives of mountain search from transient hardware platforms.

Designed during journeys through the mountains of Scotland and Wales, this system treats search and rescue as a stable, solution-agnostic process. By utilizing cellular RF geolocation, edge-AI software, and physical terrain constraints, the platform is engineered to navigate Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) constraints and extreme alpine conditions[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

Outcome-Driven AI

Solving the Functional Job of SAR.

The core functional Job-to-be-Done for mountain rescue is solution-agnostic. Systems succeed when they are engineered to respect the physical, environmental, and human realities of alpine environments[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).

Execution Strategy
  1. Establish high-confidence search boundaries using DEM-derived terrain friction[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
  2. Minimize latency between emergency signals and coordinate registration via cellular base station emulation[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
  3. Generate collision-free UAV trajectories along vertical alpine walls[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
  4. Aggregate visual edge-computing telemetry to an incident orchestration dashboard.
